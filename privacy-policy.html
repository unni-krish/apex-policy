We only see aggregated, anonymized analytics through YouTube Studio, such as views, demographics, traffic sources and engagement. We may use the information we collect to:

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it only in these situations:

Comments you post on YouTube are public and can be seen, shared and indexed by anyone. Avoid posting personal or sensitive information in public comments.
